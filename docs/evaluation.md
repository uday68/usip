## 1. Evaluation Protocol & Ground-Truth Test Setup

To avoid optimistic data leakage, evaluation was conducted exclusively on **unseen test sites, unseen survey dates, and unseen pipeline sequences**:
- **AI4Shipwrecks**: 13 held-out shipwreck sites (`Corsair`, `Corsican`, `Monohansett`, `Viator`, `Lucinda_van_Valkenburg`, `WP_Thew`, `Monrovia`, etc.) containing 317 test instances. Zero site overlap with training data.
- **AquaScan-1K**: Surveys collected on test dates (`2025-08-28`, `2025-08-29`, `2025-11-14`) containing 97 unseen acoustic debris images.
- **SubPipeMini**: Held-out sequence chunk separated from training by an acoustic exclusion buffer.
- **Total Test Images Evaluated**: 1,014 unified acoustic sonar tiles.
- **Total Ground-Truth Target Instances**: 756 instances.

## 4. Honest Scientific Commentary on Metric Performance

1. **Why is mAP@50 0.1316 rather than an inflated 0.99?**
   - Terrestrial RGB models tested on random splits often report artificial >90% mAP because contiguous video frames or identical sites leak between splits.
   - In our evaluation, the model was tested on completely unseen shipwrecks and unseen survey days.
   - For example, evaluating on the *Corsican* shipwreck when the model only saw the *EB Allen* tests true geomorphological and acoustic hull generalization.
2. **Operational Significance of Multi-Factor Ranking**:
   - Because raw detector confidence alone cannot guarantee physical elevation, USIP couples the detector with the **Acoustic Shadow Contrast Ratio** and **Context Similarity**.
   - Even when a candidate has moderate model confidence (~0.60), a strong acoustic cast shadow ($\ge 1.6\times$ contrast) escalates its priority to **`CRITICAL`** or **`HIGH`**, preventing missed detections in operational surveys.
